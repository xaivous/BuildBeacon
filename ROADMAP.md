# Roadmap

What might come next for Build Beacon. Plans change as the mod is played; ideas and bug reports are welcome on the
Discord.

## Next

- **Wall pieces that tile.** The Boss Trophy Mount and the Trophy Panel snap to walls by their centre only for now.
  Snapping by their edges and corners comes back once it works on every kind of wall, so Trophy Panels line up edge
  to edge again.
- **Trophies that sit flush.** Some mounted trophies stand a little off their alcove or hook, by a different gap for
  each trophy. They will sit against it.

## The Deep North

The Deep North has no boss trophy today, and it is not certain the game will ever add one. If it does, a Deep North
boss trophy will make that biome's building materials free (or 95% off, by the server's choice), like the other
bosses' trophies do.
