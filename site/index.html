Discount Matrix

Every trophy in the game against every material the Build Beacon discounts. A number is the discount level that creature trophy gives the material; Free marks the materials a boss trophy covers: free by default, or 95% off on a server that sets BossPercent = 95. A beacon adds up the levels of the trophies in its network, to the top level. Tick the trophies you have to see what your beacon would do, or load your server's rules files to see its matrix instead of the defaults.

Your trophies

Tick trophies in the table below (the box beside each name) to add them up the way a beacon does.

Your server's rules

A server can change what each trophy does. Paste the contents of its BepInEx/config/BuildBeacon.BossRules.txt and BuildBeacon.MobRules.txt (or pick the files), and the matrix and your totals use them. Lines are read the way the mod reads them: Trophy | Material for bosses, Trophy | Material | Level or Trophy | Stackable for creatures, # starts a comment. Level percents and Boss percent are the LevelPercents and BossPercent settings in its BepInEx/config/xaivous.buildbeacon.cfg.

Levels. Levels from different trophies add up in one beacon, up to the top level; each creature trophy counts once unless the rules mark it Stackable. Where beacons overlap, the one that takes the most off counts (the default Stacking = Max). The Max level row at the bottom is the most creature trophies alone can do for each material, with one of every trophy that has a rule for it.

Rare marks mini-bosses, Hildir's chest bosses, rare spawns and creatures from a single cave or dungeon. † The biome is placed from the game's names and descriptions rather than spawn data.

Balance view. The footer adds the levels as if one of each trophy were slotted in one beacon. Each creature rule gives one level, and no material has more than three trophies. Construction woods and stones (bold 2/3) aim for level 2 from the common creature trophies and 3 with a rare one; powerful materials (metals, cores, crystal, Dvergr parts) stop at level 2; materials only a rare creature drops aim for 1 from its trophy; every other creature-discounted material aims for 1 from common trophies. A dashed outline marks a miss or a level past the cap.
