## 1. What this is

A Valheim 1.0 mod (BepInEx + Jötunn 2.30.2, net48) adding the **Build Beacon** and two **boss trophy holders**.

- **Beacon.** Slot creature ("mob") trophies into it for small discounts on their materials inside its radius. It has
  its own model with four alcoves that show its trophies, a trophy panel with Trophies and Discounts tabs, readable
  rules files synced from the server, and refunds that honour what was actually paid.
- **Boss trophy holders.** "Boss Trophy Pillar" (`xai_beacon_bossholder_pillar`) and "Boss Trophy Mount"
  (`xai_beacon_bossholder_wall`, wall-only) each hold one boss trophy, hung on a hook. They are vanilla crafting
  station extensions of the beacon: placed within 6 m, joined by the yellow connection thread, linked to the closest
  beacon. A boss trophy on a linked holder makes its biome's materials free inside the beacon's radius.
- **Level and radius.** Beacon level = 1 + linked holders (with or without a trophy), up to 8. Radius = 30 m + 10 m
  per level above 1, so 100 m at level 8. Mob slots grow with counted boss trophies.

## 5. Gotchas that cost real time (all handled, all recurrable)

- **`WearNTear.m_noSupportWear = true` means "requires support".** A stone-material piece on wood collapses silently.
  The wall mount uses Wood as its support material for that reason (its look comes from its materials).
- **One bad Harmony patch aborts `PatchAll`.** Check signatures with sigdump first (`tools/sigdump` needs SDK 10; if it
  is missing, build a net8.0 copy outside the repo).
- **Saved config values beat new defaults.** Changing a default does nothing for existing cfg files; use a new key
  (done for `RadiusAtLevel1`/`RadiusPerLevel`/`MaxLevel`).
- **Player takes the first Interactable/Hoverable on an object.** The beacon's `CraftingStation` is added after
  `BeaconController`, the holders' `StationExtension` after `BossHolder`, so ours win. The log confirms the beacon's.
- **A dedicated server never loads pieces** and drops RPCs addressed to unloaded objects; the ZDO owner is the only
  writer. Ownership is not the creator; the server hands it to a nearby player.
- **Vanilla wood walls snap on their centre plane** (0.30 m thick); stone and marble walls snap on their faces. The
  wall mount's snap points sit on its back face, so it sinks into wood walls when snapped (kept as is).
- **Vanilla hides zero-cost requirement rows**; ours are re-shown by a postfix. **With no container open,
  Ctrl+click in the inventory drops the stack**; our patch takes it over while the panel is open.
- **Jötunn buttons play two sounds per click** (`ButtonSfx` select on mouse down, click on mouse up); `BeaconUI.Quiet`.
- **Valheim's tonemapping turns bright pastel emission white.** Keep emission saturated, intensity ≈ 2.
- **Copied vanilla materials carry keywords** (vertex noise, parallax); the plugin disables them. Materials are
  dressed only while still on Standard, so shared materials are dressed once.
- **A piece prefab's first renderer is often a snow overlay or LOD**: use `prefab:<name>/<material>` tokens.
- **Bundle mesh data is Z-up**; the `model` child is rotated -90° about X. Blender -Y is Unity +Z (front).
- **Unity project DLL metas** have Auto Reference and Validate References off; new DLLs need pre-written metas.
  `Mono.Cecil.dll` stays, player-only. The player type scan only reruns when its inputs change.
- **MCP**: `Unity_RunCommand` "Unity not detected" → `GetState`, retry once. Blender `bpy` text edits do not mark the
  file dirty; the user saves and commits `totem.blend` themselves. The Blender viewport ignores `view_distance`
  changes until a redraw; `jump_to_view3d_object_by_name` or `area.tag_redraw()` helps.
- **Rider once reverted the csproj**, dropping `EmbeddedResource`; `build-and-verify.sh` checks the manifest.
- **The `[diag]` Remove/Destroy warnings fire on every hammer removal**; expected.

## 6. Decisions made, don't relitigate without the user

- One DLL; helpers in `BuildBeacon/Util`. Server's rules files are authoritative, synced via Jötunn admin-only config.
- Boss trophies live on holders linked as vanilla station extensions (beacon = CraftingStation, holders =
  StationExtension, stacking). Beacons take only mob trophies; bosses slotted before holders existed keep counting and
  can be removed, not added. Each boss counts once per beacon, up to BossSlots (8), in progression order.
- Level = 1 + linked holders (with or without a trophy), max 8; radius 30 m + 10 m per level. Mob slots and the boss
  cap still follow counted boss trophies.
- Two holder pieces with identical behaviour and their own models; IDs `xai_beacon_bossholder_pillar` and `_wall`.
- Duplicate mob trophies stack (flat and percent add per copy); mob discounts stop at MinCost; bosses make materials
  free. The Discounts tab says so.
- Slot changes follow vanilla ownership (Container open handshake for the panel, ItemStand RequestOwn for using a
  trophy); edits are ward-protected; discounts apply to everyone in the radius.
- No `Container` on the beacon; the player's inventory opens beside the panel with a Ctrl+click patch instead.
- Trophies: boss first in the alcoves, Mounted placement by default (config `TrophyPlacement`).
- The beacon keeps the wisp torch's hum. Refund default `PaidCost`.
- Blender edits only through the generator scripts. Crown vocabulary: cap, abacus, capital, cornice ring, band, runes.
- Commits only when the user says "commit"; descriptive messages; unrelated work in separate commits (split a file's
  changes by resetting it and reapplying the edit scripts for one change at a time).
- MCP servers are project-scoped and committed (`.mcp.json`).
